Takeaway: 512 huge regions fit STLB → ~800 walks; 262k 4k pages + random → ~50M 4k walks (same order as 4 GiB run).

Conclusions:
- As huge pages get to around 500, they start getting into l2 TLB of 4k entries.
- 2MB page walks take less time 25% compared to 4k page walks as one level less transitioned.
- Think of the STLB not as one long shelf with 4k open slots, but as 2048 slots organized into, say, 
256 mailboxes, each with 8 pigeonholes (that's what "256 sets, 8-way associative" means). 
When a new page translation comes in, it doesn't get to pick any empty slot anywhere — hardware math 
(usually a few bits of the virtual address) decides which one mailbox it's allowed to go into. 
It can only use one of that mailbox's 8 pigeonholes. Now connect it to your experiment. With random access, you're not filling mailboxes in a neat, evenly-spread way
